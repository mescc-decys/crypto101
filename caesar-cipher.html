Caesar Cipher Lab

What this is for

A Caesar cipher hides a message by moving every letter the same number of places along the alphabet. That number is called the shift. This page lets you practise breaking the cipher, meaning you find the shift without being told it.

Use it in class to see two ways of doing that:

  • Guess and check: try shifts one at a time until the message makes sense (steps 1 and 3).
  • Count letters: the most common letter in the message probably stands for E (step 2).

Tip for teachers: let students work by hand first, then use the page to check their answers.
